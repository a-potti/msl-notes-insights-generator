# Injection site reactions with subcutaneous zoltarimab: pooled safety analysis

**ECCO Congress 2026, Vienna**

**Background.** Ulcerative colitis remains a chronic relapsing condition in which a
substantial proportion of patients do not achieve or maintain remission on available
advanced therapies. VELTRAXA (zoltarimab) is an
anti-TL1A monoclonal antibody approved for moderate-to-severe ulcerative colitis (UC) in adults.

**Methods.** This analysis drew on the AURORA-1 dataset. Endpoints
were assessed by central reading where applicable. Comparisons are descriptive unless
otherwise stated.

**Results.** Injection site reactions were reported in 9.4% of patients, were mild or moderate in 96% of cases, and led to discontinuation in 0.6%.

**Conclusions.** These findings inform the clinical use of zoltarimab in
moderate-to-severe ulcerative colitis (UC) in adults and identify areas requiring further study.
