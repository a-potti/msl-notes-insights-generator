Two-year maintenance of clinical and endoscopic remission with zoltarimab

M. Haddad, R. Okafor, S. Lindqvist, et al.

Abstract

Of 498 patients entering the open-label extension, 61.2% maintained clinical remission and 44.8% maintained endoscopic improvement at week 104.

Results

Steroid-free clinical remission at week 104 was 52.4%. Discontinuation for adverse events occurred in 4.9% of patients over the two-year period.

EndpointWeek 52Week 104
Clinical remission68.1%61.2%
Endoscopic improvement51.3%44.8%

Conclusions

Remission was durable through two years.
